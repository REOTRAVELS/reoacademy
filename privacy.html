Privacy Policy

What happens to the information you share with us — and the choices you have.

Effective 20 September 2026 · REO Travel Academy — a programme of REO Travels & Tour

← Back to REO Travel Academy

This policy explains what happens to the information you share with us — when you submit an application form or use the live chat — how we use it, and the choices you have. We only collect what we need to run admissions and answer you.

01 Information we collect

Our programmes are for adults aged 18 and above.

Application form. When you apply for a course we collect:

  • Your first name, middle name (optional), and last name
  • Phone number and email address
  • Your preferred course and preferred intake (Winter, Spring, Summer, or Fall)
  • Whether you would be available for physical classes
  • Your confirmation that you agree to the registration terms (including that the registration fee is non-refundable)

Live chat. We ask for your first name before you can send your first message — nothing else. Your name and messages are saved so we can reply and keep a record. Your name is also remembered in your own browser (local storage) so you are not asked again next visit; clearing your browser's site data removes it.

No tracking. We do not use advertising trackers or analytics cookies, and we do not build profiles of visitors.

02 How we use your information

  • To process your application, confirm your spot, and send onboarding details for your chosen course
  • To answer your live-chat messages and any questions you send us
  • To contact you about intakes, registration, and payment of the registration fee
  • To keep internal records of applications and conversations

03 Where it is stored & who handles it

Your information is handled only by the services we use to run this site:

  • Google (Apps Script / Google Workspace) — receives your application by email and stores chat history in a spreadsheet on our Drive. Google's Privacy Policy
  • WhatsApp (Meta) — live-chat messages are relayed to the academy's WhatsApp so a staff member can reply. WhatsApp's Privacy Policy
We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to anyone.

04 How long we keep it

Application details and chat records are kept for as long as they are useful for admissions and follow-up. You may ask us at any time to delete your information and we will remove it from our active records.

05 Your rights & choices

  • You can ask what information we hold about you
  • You can ask us to correct or delete it
  • You can use the chat with only a first name, or contact us by email instead if you prefer not to chat

Simply email info.academy@reotravelsandtours.org and we will respond.

06 Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information — access is limited to our staff and the services listed above. Like any internet transmission, no system can be guaranteed 100% secure, so please avoid sharing sensitive documents through the chat.

07 Changes to this policy

We may update this page from time to time. The current version and effective date will always be shown here.
