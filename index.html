Do I need prior travel industry experience?+

No. Our programmes are built for both newcomers and current travel consultants — the Diploma starts from the fundamentals before moving into operations.

Diploma vs. Certification — what's the difference?+

The Diploma is our full 8-course, 10-week programme covering the whole business. The Certifications are focused 3–4 week programmes on one area — customer relations or HR.

Can I attend online if I'm not in Lagos?+

Yes — every programme runs online & physical, so you can join live sessions from anywhere or attend in person if you're local.

What happens after I register?+

You'll get a confirmation with payment and onboarding details for the next cohort. You can also message us on WhatsApp with any questions first.

Is there a payment plan?+

Reach out to us directly — we're happy to discuss a plan that works for your intake.

Will this help me start my own travel business?+

Yes — the Diploma covers entrepreneurship and business management alongside the operational skills, for people planning to start their own agency.
